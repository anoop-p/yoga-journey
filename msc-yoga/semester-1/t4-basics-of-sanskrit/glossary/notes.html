Glossary: Sanskrit to English

Every Sanskrit term met so far in Unit 1, with the meaning in English.

Use this page to look up a word fast. The Sanskrit word is given in Roman with marks (IAST), then in Devanagari, then the meaning. New terms are added as more classes are covered. For the letters themselves, see the varnamala chart.

Honest note. The meanings follow the Unit 1 class notes class. The Devanagari spellings of the terms and the word-by-word meanings (for example "touching" for sparśa) are standard grammar general. Check them against your teacher's sheet before the exam.
