01 · Unit

Shri Shyama Charan Lahiri

Kriya yoga for householders.

Born 1828 in Bengal, mahasamadhi 1895. Kriya yoga master, disciple of Mahavatar Babaji (met near Ranikhet). Worked as an accountant in the military engineering department; a householder. Taught kriya to sincere seekers. Disciples: Panchanon Bhattacharya, Swami Sri Yukteswar Giri, whose disciple was Paramahansa Yogananda. class

02 · Unit

Shri T. Krishnamacharya

Ashtanga vinyasa and the Mysore line.

1888-1989, teacher, Ayurvedic healer, scholar, called the father of modern yoga (or the reviver of Hatha yoga). Guru: Ramamohana Brahmachari. Taught at the Mysore palace. Influenced by the Sri Tattvanidhi (122 asanas). class

  • Ashtanga Vinyasa: breath coordination, asana, vinyasa, drishti, flow; different from Patanjali's ashtanga
  • Two surya namaskars: Vaidika (A, 10 poses) and Yogika (B)
  • Books: Yoga Makaranda, Yoga Sanagalu, Nathamuni's Yoga Rahasya, Yogavalli, Yoga Yajnavalkya
  • Disciples: K. Pattabhi Jois (Yoga Mala), B.K.S. Iyengar (Light on Yoga; his sister was Krishnamacharya's wife), Indra Devi, T.K.V. Desikachar (son; The Heart of Yoga)

Kuvalayananda (1883-1966, Jagannath Ganesh Gune, guru Swami Madhav Das): began scientific research on yoga 1920, journal Yoga Mimamsa 1924, founded Kaivalyadhama at Lonavala 1924. class

Honest note. The "father of modern yoga" title: the Yogis document gives it to Krishnamacharya. Some books give it to Vivekananda. Write what your teacher says.

03 · Unit

Swami Sivananda Saraswati

Service and synthesis.

1887-1963, born Kuppuswami in Tamil Nadu, a doctor who practised in Malaya before sannyasa. Founded the Divine Life Society, Rishikesh (1936) and the Yoga Vedanta Forest Academy (1948). Over 200 books. Teaching: Serve, love, give, purify, meditate, realise. Combined karma, bhakti, raja and jnana. Sivananda yoga is a gentle Hatha for health. class

Disciples: Swami Vishnudevananda (International Sivananda Yoga Vedanta Centres), Swami Satyananda (Bihar School of Yoga 1963, Yoga Nidra), Swami Satchidananda (Integral Yoga Institute). Books: Bliss Divine, Practice of Yoga, Science of Pranayama, Kundalini Yoga. class

Honest note. Conflict. His guru is "Swami Vishwananda Saraswati" in your class note and "Swami Vishvesharananda" in the Yogis document. I could not settle it; use your teacher's version.

04 · Unit

Swami Rama of the Himalayas

Science and spirit.

1925-1996, born in the Garhwal Himalaya, doctor, guru Bengali Baba. Founded the Himalayan Institute of Yoga Science and Philosophy; joined traditional yoga to modern science. Took part in Menninger Foundation research in 1970 (voluntary control of heart, temperature, brain waves). Books: Living with the Himalayan Masters, Path of Fire and Light, Science of Breath, Art of Joyful Living, Meditation and Its Practice. class

05 · Unit

Maharishi Mahesh Yogi

Transcendental meditation.

1918-2008, a physicist (born Chhattisgarh per your class note), guru Swami Brahmananda Saraswati. Founded Transcendental Meditation: silent mantra repetition to settle the mind, taught through authorised centres. Interest in stress, brain activity and consciousness; Science of Creative Intelligence; Maharishi International University. Books: Science of Being and Art of Living, Love and God. class

06 · Unit

Pt. Shri Ram Sharma Acharya

Gayatri sadhana and social change.

1911-1990, born at Anwalkheda, Agra. Teaching: individual transformation, then family, then society. Gayatri sadhana (japa, meditation, prayer, self-discipline), yajna as selflessness, magazine Akhand Jyoti, All World Gayatri Pariwar, Shantikunj at Haridwar, Yug Nirman Yojana (new era), scientific spirituality. class
