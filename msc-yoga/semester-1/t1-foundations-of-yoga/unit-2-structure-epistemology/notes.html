01 · Unit

Raja yoga

The royal path of Patanjali.

Raja means royal. The system is Patanjala Yoga Darshana; "raja yoga" is a later name (Vivekananda). Yoga Sutra has about 195 sutras in four padas: Samadhi, Sadhana, Vibhuti, Kaivalya. class

Definition YS 1.2: yogash chitta-vritti-nirodhah. Aim: stilling the vrittis, then discrimination of purusha and prakriti, then kaivalya. class

PartContent
StructureAbhyasa + vairagya (1.12); kriya yoga: tapas, svadhyaya, ishvara-pranidhana (2.1); eight limbs
Eight limbsYama, niyama, asana, pranayama, pratyahara, dharana, dhyana, samadhi
YamaAhimsa, satya, asteya, brahmacharya, aparigraha
NiyamaShaucha, santosha, tapas, svadhyaya, ishvara-pranidhana
EpistemologyPramana (pratyaksha, anumana, agama), viparyaya, vikalpa, nidra, smriti: five vrittis, klishta or aklishta
MetaphysicsPurusha, prakriti, 3 gunas, chitta, five kleshas (avidya root, asmita, raga, dvesha, abhinivesha), Ishvara
02 · Unit

Jnana yoga

The path of knowledge.

Jnana is knowledge of the self. Question: who am I? Flow: avidya, wrong identification (I am the body), attachment and suffering, self-enquiry, knowledge, moksha. Example: Ramana Maharshi. Source: Upanishads, Gita, Brahma Sutra. class

PartContent
Sadhana chatushtayaViveka, vairagya, shat-sampatti, mumukshutva
Shat-sampattiShama, dama, uparati, titiksha, shraddha, samadhana
MethodShravana, manana, nididhyasana; neti neti
EpistemologyPratyaksha, anumana, shabda (shruti)
MetaphysicsAtman, jagat, Brahman, avidya, moksha
MahavakyasTat tvam asi (Chandogya), Aham Brahmasmi (Brihadaranyaka), Ayam Atma Brahma (Mandukya)
03 · Unit

Bhakti yoga

Love, devotion and surrender.

Bhakti comes from the root bhaj. It is love, devotion and surrender to the divine. In karma yoga the key is giving up the fruit; in jnana yoga, questioning; here, surrender. class

PartContent
TextsGita ch 12, Bhagavata Purana, Narada Bhakti Sutra, Shandilya Bhakti Sutra
StructureShraddha, bhakti, smarana, seva, sharanagati, divine love, moksha
Navavidha bhaktiShravana, kirtana, smarana, padasevana, archana, vandana, dasya, sakhya, atma-nivedana (Prahlada)
TypesSaguna (with form) and nirguna (formless)
BhavasShanta, dasya, sakhya, vatsalya (Putana), madhurya (Meera)
EpistemologyShraddha, shabda, anubhava
MetaphysicsJiva, Ishvara, jagat, maya, moksha

Jiva and Ishvara: Advaita (Shankara) atman is Brahman; Vishishtadvaita (Ramanuja) soul is part of the divine; Dvaita (Madhva) soul and God differ. class

Narada Bhakti Sutra: 84 sutras. Bhakti is supreme love (sa tvasmin parama prema rupa), is amrita, is greater than karma and jnana because of its result. Gauni (secondary) bhakti is of three kinds by guna; mukhya (primary) is para bhakti. Eleven forms of attachment: guna-mahatmya, rupa, puja, smarana, dasya, sakhya, vatsalya, kanta, atma-nivedana, tanmaya, parama-viraha. class

04 · Unit

Karma yoga

Action without attachment to results.

Karma yoga: doing duty without attachment to the fruit, offering action to the divine and keeping equanimity. Source: Gita (Krishna to Arjuna, ch 3). Problem is attachment, not action. class

Verses: 2.47 (right to action, not fruit), 2.48 samatvam yoga, 2.50 yogah karmasu kaushalam, 3.9. class

Chain: karma, svadharma, nishkama karma, ishvara-pranidhana, samatva, chitta-shuddhi, jnana, moksha. class

Karma types (very important)Meaning
SanchitaAccumulated from past
PrarabdhaAlready bearing fruit in this life
Agami / kriyamanaBeing made now, fruit later

Sakama karma is for a result; nishkama karma is without attachment. Three sadhakas: Hanuman (uttama), Arjuna (madhyama), Dhritarashtra (mandha, as in your note). Epistemology: pratyaksha, anumana, shabda; leads to chitta-shuddhi. class

05 · Unit

Hatha yoga

Ha and tha: sun and moon.

Ha is sun (pingala, prana), tha is moon (ida, apana): balancing the two. Developed by the Nath yogis, Matsyendranath and Gorakshanath. Svatmarama wrote the Hatha Yoga Pradipika. Hatha is a body-mind path that prepares for raja yoga: "kevalam raja yogaya hatha vidya upadishyate". class

PartContent
Ten techniquesShatkarma, mitahara, asana, mudra, bandha, pranayama, pratyahara, dharana, dhyana, samadhi
TextsGoraksha Samhita and Shataka, Siddha Siddhanta Paddhati, Hatha Pradipika (Svatmarama), Gheranda Samhita, Shiva Samhita, Hatha Ratnavali, Tirumandiram
PrincipleStable body, regulated breath, steady mind, meditation
NadisIda (left, lunar), pingala (right, solar), sushumna (central)

Hatha is not just exercise; the body is an instrument for mental and spiritual change. class

06 · Unit

Mantra yoga

Sound, repetition, meaning.

Man is mind, tra is protection. Mantra yoga: repeat sacred sounds to concentrate and purify the mind. Mantra, japa, concentration, purification, one-pointed mind, meditation, higher awareness. class

PartContent
Types of mantraBija (om, hrim, shrim, krim); saguna (om namah shivaya); nirguna (soham)
JapaVachika (aloud), upamshu (whisper), manasa (mental)
Mala108 beads
OmPranava. YS 1.27 tasya vachakah pranavah; 1.28 tajjapas tad-artha-bhavanam
EpistemologyShabda from the guru, then repetition and contemplation
MetaphysicsShabda, nada, om, shakti, Ishvara
TextsMandukya and Prashna Upanishads, Yoga Sutra, HYP
07 · Unit

Laya yoga

Mind dissolving into its source.

Laya is dissolution or absorption: the mind dissolving into its source. Close to Hatha, Nada yoga and Tantra; no single fixed system. Path: withdrawal, concentration, meditation, nada, absorption (laya), samadhi. class

Methods: nada meditation, mantra, breath awareness, concentration, meditation, kundalini practices. Nada: external, inner, subtle sound. Texts: HYP, Gheranda Samhita, Shiva Samhita, Yoga-tattva Upanishad. class

08 · Unit

Kundalini yoga

The awakening of the coiled energy.

From your handwritten assignment of 13 Sept 2026. Kundalini sleeps coiled at muladhara and is guided up to sahasrara, union with Shiva. Classical sources classify it under Laya yoga within the four: Mantra, Hatha, Laya, Raja. Tantra claims both bhukti (worldly enjoyment) and mukti (liberation). class

PartContent
HistoryGoraksha Shataka (11-12 c.) teaches sarasvati-chalana; Yoga-Kundalini Upanishad; Hatha Pradipika (15 c.); Sat-Chakra-Nirupana (16 c.)
StructureNadis (72,000 to over 300,000), ida, pingala, sushumna; seven chakras; three granthis: Brahma, Vishnu, Rudra; shakti static and dynamic
MethodApana raised by mula bandha meets agni and prana; four kumbhakas: suryabheda, ujjayi, sitali, bhastrika; three bandhas; six shatkarmas; mudras khechari, vajroli, shakti-chalana; shaktipat from the guru
EpistemologyExperience: involuntary bandhas, breath suspension, inner sound, visions, electric sensations, under a guru
MetaphysicsShakti and Shiva as two poles of one reality; body as microcosm; viras and divya sadhaka
VariantKashmir Shaivism (Trika, Spanda): shakti as ceaseless pulsation, vijnana bhairava 112 dharanas, states of waking, dream, sleep, turiya
