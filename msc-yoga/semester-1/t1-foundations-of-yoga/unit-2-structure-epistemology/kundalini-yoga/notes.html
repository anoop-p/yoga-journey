1AMeaning

Kundalini Yoga is the path concerned with awakening a dormant primordial energy, described in the Yoga-Kundalini Upanishad and the Sat-Chakra-Nirupana as coiled like a serpent at the Muladhara chakra (base of the spine), and guided upward until it unites with Shiva-consciousness at Sahasrara, the crown centre. Kundalini assignment

Know this
  • Classical sources classify it as a form of Laya Yoga within the fourfold scheme of Mantra, Hatha, Laya and Raja Yoga. Kundalini assignment
  • Unlike Jnana Yoga (liberation by intellectual discrimination), it works through the actual transformation of psychophysical energy, treating the body as the primary instrument of realisation. Kundalini assignment
  • Distinctive goal: the Tantric tradition claims both bhukti (worldly enjoyment) and mukti (liberation) together, not liberation through renunciation alone. Kundalini assignment

02 · Historical and philosophical background

Goraksha Shataka to Sat-Chakra-Nirupana

Lineage, Tantric home, contrast with Raja and Vedanta.

2AHistorical lineage

Goraksha Shataka (11th-12th c.)→Yoga-Kundalini Upanishad→Hatha Yoga Pradipika (15th c.)→Sat-Chakra-Nirupana (16th c.)

The earliest datable technique for raising Kundalini is in the Goraksha Shataka, a Nath text attributed to Gorakhnath, teaching Sarasvati-chalana (stimulating the Sarasvati nadi near the navel). It reappears in the Yoga-Kundalini Upanishad. The Sat-Chakra-Nirupana gives the most detailed classical account of the chakra system. Kundalini assignment

2BPhilosophical background and contrast

Home tradition: Tantra, where Shakti is worshipped as the Divine Mother and awakened through ritual and devotion towards union with Sadashiva. The Hatha Yoga Pradipika teaches the same awakening through discipline. Kundalini assignment

03 · Structure

Nadis, Chakras and Granthis

The framework.

3AThe framework: nadis, chakras, granthis

ElementNotes say
NadisSubtle channels, 72,000 to over 300,000 depending on source. Principal three: Ida, Pingala, Sushumna. Sushumna runs along the spinal axis (Merudanda), the pathway Kundalini must ascend
ChakrasSeven along this axis, Muladhara to Sahasrara; in the Sat-Chakra-Nirupana each is linked to a corresponding plexus of the nervous system
GranthisThree knots obstructing the ascent: Brahma, Vishnu, Rudra, formed through the three gunas; Kundalini pierces them in sequence

Seven chakras, in order, as commonly listed: Muladhara, Svadhishthana, Manipura, Anahata, Vishuddha, Ajna, Sahasrara. general

Static-dynamic polarity of Shakti

Kundalini at rest in Muladhara is the static pole; Prana circulating through the body is her dynamic expression. Practice converts static into dynamic and draws it towards the crown. Kundalini assignment

Note

These are traditional yogic concepts, not anatomical structures recognised by modern biomedical science (as your Laya notes also say). The nervous-system plexus correlation is what the Sat-Chakra-Nirupana account is described as giving, per your assignment. Kundalini assignment Laya notes

04 · Method

Bandhas, Kumbhakas and Shaktipat

How the awakening is done.

4AMechanics of awakening (Yoga-Kundalini Upanishad)

Apana raised by Mulabandha→Unites with Agni→Rises to meet Prana→Kundalini straightens→Pierces Brahma-granthi→Vishnu-granthi (heart)→Rudra-granthi (eyebrow centre)→Sahasrara

05 · Epistemology

Embodied Verification

Experience over scripture.

5AEpistemology

Knowledge is primarily experiential rather than scriptural. Classical sources list physiological and psychological signs confirming awakening: involuntary bandhas, spontaneous breath suspension, inner sound and visions, and sensations resembling electric current. Kundalini assignment

Contrast with Jnana

This is embodied verification, distinct from the scriptural testimony stressed in Jnana Yoga. Validity rests on the practitioner’s own bodily and psychic experience, ordinarily confirmed under a Guru’s guidance rather than by reasoning alone. Kundalini assignment

06 · Metaphysics

Shakti and Shiva

Two poles, the sadhakas, and the Kashmir Shaiva variant.

6AMetaphysics

The path investigates the relationship between Shakti (dynamic energy and matter) and Shiva (static, unmanifested consciousness): two poles of a single reality. Kundalini assignment

Know this
  • Totality: unlike meditation-based yoga where only the mind attains stillness, Kundalini Yoga unites body, mind and the body’s central power at Sahasrara. Kundalini assignment
  • Two practitioners: Vira sadhaka (outward ritual means) and Divya sadhaka (unites Shakti and Shiva purely within). Both attain bhukti and mukti by different routes. Kundalini assignment
  • Microcosm: the body mirrors the cosmic macrocosm; the ascent re-enacts the return of manifest energy to its unmanifest source, culminating in supreme bliss and final liberation. Kundalini assignment

6BPhilosophical variant: Kashmir Shaivism

Trika school: authorities include the Malinivijayottara Tantra and Siddhayogeshvarimata; the Spanda school rests on Vasugupta’s Shiva Sutras and the Spanda Karika. Here Shakti is Spanda, the ceaseless pulsation of consciousness itself, already present rather than a latent power triggered once. The Vijnana Bhairava Tantra’s 112 dharanas include techniques for Kundalini’s rise by concentration alone, without the asana, bandha and pranayama apparatus of Hatha. Its teachers describe the rise as a shift across states of consciousness: waking, dreaming, deep sleep, turiya, not the popular six-chakra model. Treat it as a separate philosophical perspective, not an extension of the structure above. Kundalini assignment
