1APhilosophy and the four areas

Indian philosophy is darshana (root drish, “to see”): a way of seeing or realising reality. Four areas organise the inquiry: Darshana notes

AreaCentral questionYoga’s concern
EpistemologyHow do we know? What is valid knowledge and what is error?Pramanas, the five vrittis, avidya and viveka-khyati
MetaphysicsWhat is real? What are consciousness, mind and matter?Purusha, Prakriti, gunas, chitta, Ishvara, kleshas, karma
EthicsHow should we live?Yamas, niyamas, kriya yoga, brahmaviharas
LiberationHow can suffering end?Kaivalya through ashtanga yoga, viveka-khyati and samadhi
Overview

Epistemology (how we know) + Metaphysics (what is real) + Ethics (how to live) = the foundation; Liberation (kaivalya) = the goal; Ashtanga yoga = the path. Darshana notes

1BYoga Darshana

Know this
  • One of the six schools, systematised by Patanjali in the Yoga Sutra (about 195 sutras in four padas: Samadhi, Sadhana, Vibhuti, Kaivalya). Darshana notes
  • Shares its theory with Samkhya but adds Ishvara and a detailed practical method. Samkhya stresses liberating knowledge (viveka-jnana); Yoga stresses the disciplined practice that makes it stable. Darshana notes
  • YS 1.2: yogash chitta-vritti-nirodhah. YS 1.3: then the Seer abides in its own nature. Darshana notes

03 · Core ideas: ethics

Yamas, Niyamas and the Yogic Ethic

Where ethics sits in the system and what is distinctive about it.

3AEthics in Yoga

Know this
  • Ethics is not an optional add-on: yamas and niyamas are the first two limbs and the base of a steady mind. They reduce raga, dvesha and agitation that distort perception, and organise one’s relation to others and oneself. Darshana notes
  • Pratipaksha bhavana (YS 2.33): cultivate opposites of harmful thoughts. Kriya yoga (YS 2.1): tapas, svadhyaya, Ishvara-pranidhana. Brahmaviharas (YS 1.33): maitri, karuna, mudita, upeksha. Darshana notes
Yama (YS 2.30)Fruit when established
AhimsaHostility is given up in one’s presence (2.35)
SatyaWords and actions become effective (2.36)
AsteyaWealth and resources come (2.37)
BrahmacharyaVigour and vitality (2.38)
AparigrahaUnderstanding of the “why” of existence (2.39)

Darshana notes Niyamas (YS 2.32): shaucha, santosha, tapas, svadhyaya, Ishvara-pranidhana. Mahavrata (YS 2.31): the yamas are the “great vow” when universal, not limited by birth, place, time or circumstance.

3BFeatures of yogic ethics

Remember
  • Linked to liberation: ethical purification is a means to a steady, discerning mind. Darshana notes
  • Psychological as well as social: concerned with inner states (kleshas) and outward behaviour. Darshana notes
  • Universal: not restricted by community or occasion (mahavrata). Darshana notes
  • Applied to asana practice: non-violence towards the body (not forcing range or enduring sharp pain), truthfulness about limits, contentment, non-competition. Darshana notes

04 · Core ideas: liberation

Kaivalya and the Other Schools

Bondage, samadhi, liberation compared across schools, and the everyday practice link.

4ABondage and liberation

Know this
  • Bondage: conjunction (samyoga) of Seer and seen due to avidya (YS 2.17, 2.24). Darshana notes
  • Kaivalya: cessation of the conjunction when avidya is removed (YS 2.25); YS 4.34, the gunas have no further purpose for Purusha. It is not destruction of Purusha, nor union with a higher entity: it is realisation of what Purusha always is. Darshana notes
Kind of samadhiDescription
SamprajnataWith cognition; stages vitarka, vichara, ananda, asmita (YS 1.17)
AsamprajnataBeyond cognitive content; latent impressions remain (YS 1.18)
NirbijaSeedless; impressions cease (YS 1.51)
DharmameghaCloud of virtue after perfect discrimination (YS 4.29-30)

Darshana notes Obstacles (YS 1.30) and aids: see the Raja Yoga page, method section.

4BLiberation across schools

SchoolView of liberationMeans

4CLiberation and everyday practice

Know this
  • The path starts practically: ethical living, a healthy steady body (asana), regulated breath, a calm mind. Darshana notes
  • These have well-recognised effects on health, but in Yoga Darshana they aim at removing the root causes of suffering (kleshas) and gaining discriminative knowledge. Distinguish benefits of practice (stress reduction, flexibility, balance) from the philosophical goal (kaivalya). Darshana notes

05 · Exam answers

Model Answers

Short answers; the long answers are on the Raja Yoga page.

Answers follow the four areas.

10 marksYoga darshana

Explain Yoga Darshana in terms of epistemology, metaphysics, ethics and liberation.

Tap to reveal answer

Introduction. Yoga Darshana, one of the six schools, was systematised by Patanjali (195 sutras, four padas). It shares Samkhya’s theory and adds Ishvara and a practical method. Definition: yogash chitta-vritti-nirodhah.

Epistemology. Three pramanas; five vrittis; avidya as root error; viveka-khyati as liberating knowledge.

Metaphysics. Purusha and Prakriti, three gunas, chitta, Ishvara as purusha-vishesha, five kleshas, karma and samskara.

Ethics. Yamas and niyamas as the foundation, kriya yoga, brahmaviharas, mahavrata; applied to asana.

Liberation. Kaivalya through ashtanga yoga leading to viveka-khyati and nirbija samadhi.

Conclusion. Yoga integrates the four into one practical system.

Darshana notes Wording mine added

5 marksYoga darshana

Explain the ethics of Yoga Darshana.

Tap to reveal answer

Yamas (ahimsa, satya, asteya, brahmacharya, aparigraha, with fruits) and niyamas, kriya yoga, brahmaviharas and pratipaksha bhavana. Ethics is the foundation for a steady mind, is psychological and universal (mahavrata), and applies to asana practice.

5 marksYoga darshana

Compare liberation in Samkhya, Yoga, Nyaya and Advaita.

Tap to reveal answer

Samkhya: kaivalya through discriminative knowledge. Yoga: kaivalya through ashtanga yoga and nirbija samadhi. Nyaya: apavarga through tattva-jnana. Advaita: moksha as identity of Atman and Brahman through jnana.

5 marksYoga darshana

Tap to reveal answer

Samprajnata (vitarka, vichara, ananda, asmita), asamprajnata (beyond cognition), nirbija (seedless; impressions cease) and dharmamegha (cloud of virtue after perfect discrimination).
