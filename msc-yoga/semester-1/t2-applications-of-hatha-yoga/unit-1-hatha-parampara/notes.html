Understand it · what “hatha” means class
  • Ha = sun = pranic energy = pingala nadi (your notes link it to the sympathetic nervous system). Tha = moon = mental energy = ida nadi (parasympathetic).
  • Hatha yoga is balancing these two energies. When ha and tha are balanced, the energy rises in sushumna and kundalini awakens.
  • Shiva = pure consciousness; Shakti = creative energy. Nadi comes from “nad”, to flow: nadis are what flows.
  • Pradipika means “light” or “lamp” (the class explanation: the way to the light). Its four chapters, as your notes list them: asanas, shatkarma and pranayama, mudra and bandha, samadhi.
  • Purpose: hatha yoga is the ladder to raja yoga. HYP 1.67 says asanas and kumbhakas are to be practised until raja yoga is reached; 2.76 says neither works without the other. HYP book

02 · Matha Lakshana

Where and How the Hatha Yogi Practises

HYP 1.12 gives the place (the country and the practice space); 1.13 describes the hermitage (matha). Your notes mark both as “important, must study”.

  1. Small door, no windows, holes or cracks (HYP 1.13)
  2. The room itself: neither too high nor too low, spotlessly clean, free of animals and insects
  3. Raised platform in front, open with a roof (a “mandapa” or bower)
  4. Well (a water source)
  5. Surrounding wall or fence; the whole site is one bow’s length clear of rocks, fire and water (1.12)
Understand it

Verse 12 (place): the hatha yogi lives alone in a hermitage, in a space a bow’s length (about 1.5 m) with no danger from rocks, fire or water, in a well-governed, virtuous kingdom where alms are easy to get. Verse 13 (the matha): small door, no aperture, holes or cracks; not too high or low; clean; pleasant. Your Muktibodhananda commentary adds that these are guidelines for the time and climate, to be adapted today: simple, clean, practical and natural. class HYP book

03 · Mitahara, Pathya & Apathya

The Hatha Yogi’s Diet

HYP 1.57–63. Mitahara is the moderate, suitable diet; pathya foods help practice, apathya foods block it.

  1. Food: half of the stomach
  2. Water: one quarter
  3. Air: the last quarter stays free. This is the “one fourth left empty” of verse 58
Understand it · Verse 58 HYP book

Mitahara is agreeable, sweet (fresh and pleasant, not sugary) food, eaten leaving one fourth of the stomach empty, as an offering to please Shiva (the inner self). Your notes: no under-eating and no over-eating; about three-fourths full; eat with gratitude; not one meal a day, fasting, irregular meals or snacking. class

3APathya (suitable) and Apathya (unsuitable) foods

Pathya: HYP 1.62–63 HYP book

  • Whole grains: wheat, rice, barley
  • Milk and ghee; honey; brown sugar and sugar candy
  • Dry ginger; patola fruit; the “five vegetables” (leafy, spinach-like)
  • Mung and similar light pulses; pure water
  • 1.63: nourishing, sweet, with ghee and milk, nourishing the dhatus, pleasing and suitable

Apathya: HYP 1.59–61 HYP book

  • 1.59: bitter, sour, pungent, salty, heating foods; sour gruel, oil, sesame, mustard; alcohol, fish, meat; curds, buttermilk, horse gram, jujube, oil cakes; asafoetida and garlic
  • 1.60: food reheated after going cold, dry food, too much salt or acid, stale food, too many mixed vegetables
  • 1.61: fire, bad company, long pilgrimages, cold bathing at dawn in winter, fasting, tasks that cause bodily pain
Know this · why the book says so HYP book
  • Mitahara means sattwic, light, easily digested food. Heavy food leads to a tamasic state; the aim is to avoid wasting prana on digestion.
  • Milk and ghee protect the digestive lining that shatkarma can wash away, and calm stomach heat.
  • Reheated food ferments and causes indigestion, wind and acidity; mixed vegetables upset digestion.
  • Regular meals, not frequent fasting: the book says one who devotes hours to practice should not strain the body.

Source note. Your class notes just say “Yogic diet” (5 Nov) with no detail; the definitions above come from the handout and the book. HYP 1.57 also links mitahara to success: the brahmachari who takes moderate pure food and is regular becomes siddha after a year. The handout’s “three-fourths full” and the book’s “half food, quarter water, quarter air” mean the same thing.

04 · Six Causes of Failure and Success

Badhaka and Sadhaka Tattva

HYP 1.15 lists six things that destroy yoga; 1.16 lists six that bring success. Both are “important, must study” in your notes. Write the sloka, then the list.

1.15 Badhaka: six causes of failure class

  1. Atyahara: overeating
  2. Prayasa: excessive exertion (beyond capacity)
  3. Prajalpa: excessive talking
  4. Niyama agraha: excessive attachment to rules
  5. Jana sanga: excessive socialising, company of common people
  6. Laulya: unsteadiness, a wavering mind

1.16 Sadhaka: six causes of success class

  1. Utsaha: enthusiasm
  2. Sahasa: courage
  3. Dhairya: perseverance
  4. Tattva jnana: discrimination (knowing the truth)
  5. Nishchaya: unshakeable faith and firm resolve
  6. Jana sanga parityaga: avoiding the company of common people

Source note. The Sanskrit names above follow the order of the verse (utsahat, sahasat, dhairyat, tattva-jnanat, nishchayat, jana-sanga-parityagat). Your handout pairs perseverance with “Sahasa”, discrimination with “Dhairyat” and faith with “Tattva jnana”, which does not match the verse order; the English names are the same six, so for the exam the English list is safe. Confirm the pairing with your teacher if she asks for the Sanskrit names. added: confirm with teacher

Understand it · what each means HYP book
  • Why they matter: hatha yoga aims to balance ida and pingala; each cause of failure keeps the energy scattered, each cause of success gathers it.
  • Overeating and exertion burden body and prana; talking and company scatter energy and mind; rigid rules and unsteadiness are the two extremes of attitude.
  • Enthusiasm keeps regularity (“every day like the first day”); perseverance means continuing in rain, hail or shine until the goal; faith in the guru and the ultimate truth is the most important.
Memory aid

07 · Shatkriyas & Benefits

The Six Cleansing Practices (HYP 2.21–37)

Shat = six, karma or kriya = action. They remove excess fat, mucus and wind (the doshas) so that pranayama can be practised without strain. Verse 2.22 names the six.

Understand it · 2.21, 2.22, 2.36 HYP book
  • 2.21: when fat or mucus is excessive, do shatkarma before pranayama; those whose doshas are balanced need not. The doshas are kapha (mucus), pitta (bile), vata (wind).
  • 2.22: Dhauti, basti, neti, trataka, nauli, kapalabhati are the six cleansing processes.
  • 2.23: they are secret, learned only from a guru. 2.36: after the six, pranayama succeeds without strain. 2.37: some teachers say pranayama alone cleans the nadis; the book says both views are correct.

जानोर्बहिर्वेष्टित-वाम-पादम् ।
प्रगृह्य तिष्ठेत्परिवर्तिताङ्गः
श्री-मत्स्यनाथोदितमासनं स्यात् ॥

Matsyendrasana: right foot at the root of the left thigh, left foot wound outside the knee, hold it and turn the body.

verse 1.29 · book 1.27मत्स्येन्द्र-पीठं जठर-प्रदीप्तिं
प्रचण्ड-रुग्मण्डल-खण्डनास्त्रम् ।
अभ्यासतः कुण्डलिनी-प्रबोधं
चन्द्र-स्थिरत्वं च ददाति पुंसाम् ॥

Matsyendrasana kindles the digestive fire, destroys severe diseases, awakens kundalini and steadies the moon (bindu).

verse 1.30 · book 1.28प्रसार्य पादौ भुवि दण्ड-रूपौ
दोर्भ्यां पदाग्र-द्वितयं गृहीत्वा ।
जानूपरिन्यस्त-ललाट-देशो
वसेदिदं पश्चिमतानमाहुः ॥

Paschimottanasana: legs stretched like sticks, hold the toes with the hands, forehead on the knees.

verse 1.31 · book 1.29इति पश्चिमतानमासनाग्र्यं
पवनं पश्चिम-वाहिनं करोति ।
उदयं जठरानलस्य कुर्याद्
उदरे कार्श्यमरोगतां च पुंसाम् ॥

It makes the breath flow in the back channel (sushumna), kindles the digestive fire, makes the belly lean and gives health.

verse 1.32 · book 1.30धरामवष्टभ्य कर-द्वयेन
तत्-कूर्पर-स्थापित-नाभि-पार्श्वः ।
उच्चासनो दण्डवदुत्थितः खे
मायूरमेतत्प्रवदन्ति पीठम् ॥

Mayurasana: palms on the ground, elbows at the sides of the navel, body raised like a staff in the air.

verse 1.33 · book 1.31हरति सकल-रोगानाशु गुल्मोदरादीन्
अभिभवति च दोषानासनं श्री-मयूरम् ।
बहु कदशन-भुक्तं भस्म कुर्यादशेषं
जनयति जठराग्निं जारयेत्काल-कूटम् ॥

Mayurasana quickly removes gulma and abdominal diseases, overcomes imbalance of the humours, burns up unsuitable food and digests even deadly poison.

verse 1.34 · book 1.32उत्तानं शबवद्भूमौ शयनं तच्छवासनम् ।
शवासनं श्रान्ति-हरं चित्त-विश्रान्ति-कारकम् ॥

Shavasana: lying on the back like a corpse; it removes tiredness and brings rest to the mind.

verse 1.35 · book 1.33चतुरशीत्यासनानि शिवेन कथितानि च ।
तेभ्यश्चतुष्कमादाय सारभूतं ब्रवीम्यहम् ॥

Shiva taught 84 asanas; Svatmarama gives the essence of four.

verse 1.36 · book 1.34सिद्धं पद्मं तथा सिंहं भद्रं वेति चतुष्टयम् ।
श्रेष्ठं तत्रापि च सुखे तिष्ठेत्सिद्धासने सदा ॥

The four are siddha, padma, simha and bhadra; of these, always sit in siddhasana.

verse 1.37 · book 1.35योनि-स्थानकमङ्घ्रि-मूल-घटितं कृत्वा दृढं विन्यसेत्
मेण्ढ्रे पादमथैकमेव हृदये कृत्वा हनुं सुस्थिरम् ।
स्थाणुः संयमितेन्द्रियोऽचल-दृशा पश्येद्भ्रुवोरन्तरं
ह्येतन्मोक्ष-कपाट-भेद-जनकं सिद्धासनं प्रोच्यते ॥

Siddhasana: heel at the perineum, the other heel above the genitals, chin on chest, steady, senses controlled, gaze at the eyebrow centre. It opens the door of liberation.

verse 1.38 · book 1.36मेण्ढ्रादुपरि विन्यस्य सव्यं गुल्फं तथोपरि ।
गुल्फान्तरं च निक्षिप्य सिद्धासनमिदं भवेत् ॥

The second way: place the left heel above the genitals and the other heel above it.

verse 1.40 · book 1.38यमेष्विव मिताहारमहिंसा नियमेष्विव ।
मुख्यं सर्वासनेष्वेकं सिद्धाः सिद्धासनं विदुः ॥

As mitahara is the chief of the yamas and non-violence of the niyamas, siddhasana is the chief of all asanas.

verse 1.41 · book 1.39चतुरशीति-पीठेषु सिद्धमेव सदाभ्यसेत् ।
द्वासप्तति-सहस्राणां नाडीनां मल-शोधनम् ॥

It purifies the 72,000 nadis.

verse 1.42 · book 1.40आत्म-ध्यायी मिताहारी यावद्द्वादश-वत्सरम् ।
सदा सिद्धासनाभ्यासाद्योगी निष्पत्तिमाप्नुयात् ॥

A self-contemplating yogi on moderate diet who practises siddhasana for twelve years attains perfection.

verse 1.43 · book 1.41किमन्यैर्बहुभिः पीठैः सिद्धे सिद्धासने सति ।
प्राणानिले सावधाने बद्धे केवल-कुम्भके ।
उत्पद्यते निरायासात्स्वयमेवोन्मनी कला ॥

When siddhasana is perfected and the breath is held in kevala kumbhaka, the unmani state arises by itself.

verse 1.44 · book 1.42तथैकास्मिन्नेव दृढे सिद्धे सिद्धासने सति ।
बन्ध-त्रयमनायासात्स्वयमेवोपजायते ॥

From siddhasana alone the three bandhas arise without effort.

verse 1.45 · book 1.43नासनं सिद्ध-सदृशं न कुम्भः केवलोपमः ।
न खेचरी-समा मुद्रा न नाद-सदृशो लयः ॥

No asana like siddha, no kumbhaka like kevala, no mudra like khechari, no absorption like nada.

verse 1.46 · book 1.44वामोरूपरि दक्षिणं च चरणं संस्थाप्य वामं तथा
दक्षोरूपरि पश्चिमेन विधिना धृत्वा कराभ्यां दृढम् ।
अङ्गुष्ठौ हृदये निधाय चिबुकं नासाग्रमालोकयेत्
एतद्व्याधि-विनाश-कारि यमिनां पद्मासनं प्रोच्यते ॥

Padmasana: each foot on the opposite thigh, hands crossed behind holding the toes, chin on chest, gaze at the nose tip.

verse 1.49 · book 1.47इदं पद्मासनं प्रोक्तं सर्व-व्याधि-विनाशनम् ।
दुर्लभं येन केनापि धीमता लभ्यते भुवि ॥

Padmasana destroys all diseases, but is hard for most people to master.

verse 1.52 · book 1.50गुल्फौ च वृषणस्याधः सीवन्याः पार्श्वयोः क्षिपेत् ।
दक्षिणे सव्य-गुल्फं तु दक्ष-गुल्फं तु सव्यके ॥

Simhasana: ankles under the perineum at the sides of the seam, the right ankle on the left and the left on the right.

verse 1.53 · book 1.51हस्तौ तु जान्वोः संस्थाप्य स्वाङ्गुलीः सम्प्रसार्य च ।
व्यात्त-वक्त्रो निरीक्षेत नासाग्रं सुसमाहितः ॥

Hands on the knees, fingers spread, mouth open, gaze at the nose tip.

verse 1.54 · book 1.52सिंहासनं भवेदेतत्पूजितं योगि-पुङ्गवैः ।
बन्ध-त्रितय-सन्धानं कुरुते चासनोत्तमम् ॥

Simhasana is honoured by the best yogis and unites the three bandhas.

verse 1.56 · book 1.54पार्श्व-पादौ च पाणिभ्यां दृढं बद्ध्वा सुनिश्चलम् ।
भद्रासनं भवेदेतत्सर्व-व्याधि-विनाशनम् ।
गोरक्षासनमित्याहुरिदं वै सिद्ध-योगिनः ॥

Bhadrasana: hold the feet firmly with the hands and stay still. It destroys all diseases and is also called Gorakshasana.

4Mitahara, Pathya and Apathya

verse 1.59 · book 1.57ब्रह्मचारी मिताहारी त्यागी योग-परायणः ।
अब्दादूर्ध्वं भवेद्सिद्धो नात्र कार्या विचारणा ॥

A brahmachari, moderate in diet, detached and devoted to yoga, becomes a siddha after a year.

verse 1.60 · book 1.58सुस्निग्ध-मधुराहारश्चतुर्थांश-विवर्जितः ।
भुज्यते शिव-सम्प्रीत्यै मिताहारः स उच्यते ॥

Mitahara: sweet, rich food, with one fourth of the stomach left empty, eaten for the pleasure of Shiva.

verse 1.61 · book 1.59कट्वाम्ल-तीक्ष्ण-लवणोष्ण-हरीत-शाक-
सौवीर-तैल-तिल-सर्षप-मद्य-मत्स्यान् ।
आजादि-मांस-दधि-तक्र-कुलत्थकोल-
पिण्याक-हिङ्गु-लशुनाद्यमपथ्यमाहुः ॥

Apathya (to avoid): bitter, sour, pungent, salty and heating foods; sour gruel, oil, sesame, mustard, alcohol, fish, meat, curds, buttermilk, horse gram, oil cake, asafoetida, garlic.

verse 1.62 · book 1.60भोजनमहितं विद्यात्पुनरस्योष्णी-कृतं रूक्षम् ।
अतिलवणमम्ल-युक्तं कदशन-शाकोत्कं वर्ज्यम् ॥

Also unsuitable: reheated, dry, over-salted or over-sour food and bad vegetables.

verse 1.64 · book 1.62वर्जयेद्दुर्जन-प्रान्तं वह्नि-स्त्री-पथि-सेवनम् ।
प्रातः-स्नानोपवासादि काय-क्लेश-विधिं तथा ॥

Gorakhnath: avoid bad company, fire, women and long journeys, early-morning bathing and fasting, and any practice that tortures the body.

verse 1.65 · book 1.63गोधूम-शालि-यव-षाष्टिक-शोभनान्नं
क्षीराज्य-खण्ड-नवनीत-सिद्धा-मधूनि ।
शुण्ठी-पटोल-फलादिक-पञ्च-शाकं
मुद्गादि-दिव्यमुदकं च यमीन्द्र-पथ्यम् ॥

Pathya (suitable): wheat, rice, barley, milk, ghee, sugar candy, butter, honey, dried ginger, the five vegetables, mung beans, pure water.

verse 1.66 · book 1.64पुष्टं सुमधुरं स्निग्धं गव्यं धातु-प्रपोषणम् ।
मनोभिलषितं योग्यं योगी भोजनमाचरेत् ॥

The yogi should eat food that is nourishing, sweet, rich, from the cow, builds the tissues and is pleasing to the mind.

5Practice, Success and Hatha Siddhi

verse 1.67 · book 1.65युवो वृद्धोऽतिवृद्धो वा व्याधितो दुर्बलोऽपि वा ।
अभ्यासात्सिद्धिमाप्नोति सर्व-योगेष्वतन्द्रितः ॥

Young, old, very old, sick or weak: all gain siddhi by tireless practice.

verse 1.68 · book 1.66क्रिया-युक्तस्य सिद्धिः स्यादक्रियस्य कथं भवेत् ।
न शास्त्र-पाठ-मात्रेण योग-सिद्धिः प्रजायते ॥

Perfection comes to one who practises, not to one who does not; not through reading the scriptures alone.

verse 1.69 · book 1.67न वेष-धारणं सिद्धेः कारणं न च तत्-कथा ।
क्रियैव कारणं सिद्धेः सत्यमेतन्न संशयः ॥

Neither dress nor talk about yoga gives siddhi; practice alone is the cause.

verse 2.78वपुः कृशत्वं वदने प्रसन्नता
नाद-स्फुटत्वं नयने सुनिर्मले ।
अरोगता बिन्दु-जयोऽग्नि-दीपनं
नाडी-विशुद्धिर्हठ-सिद्धि-लक्षणम् ॥

The signs of hatha siddhi: lean body, tranquil face, clear inner sound, bright eyes, health, control of bindu, active digestive fire, purified nadis.

6Shatkriyas (Shatkarma)

verse 2.21मेद-श्लेष्माधिकः पूर्वं षट्-कर्माणि समाचरेत् ।
अन्यस्तु नाचरेत्तानि दोषाणां समभावतः ॥

Those with excess fat or mucus should do the six kriyas first; others need not, since their humours are balanced.

verse 2.22धौतिर्बस्तिस्तथा नेतिस्त्राटकं नौलिकं तथा ।
कपाल-भातिश्चैतानि षट्-कर्माणि प्रचक्षते ॥

The six: dhauti, basti, neti, trataka, nauli, kapalabhati.

verse 2.23कर्म षट्कमिदं गोप्यं घट-शोधन-कारकम् ।
विचित्र-गुण-सन्धाय पूज्यते योगि-पुङ्गवैः ॥

The six actions are to be kept secret; they cleanse the body and are honoured by the best yogis.

verse 2.24चतुर्-अङ्गुल-विस्तारं हस्त-पञ्च-दशायतम् ।
गुरूपदिष्ट-मार्गेण सिक्तं वस्त्रं शनैर्ग्रसेत् ।
पुनः प्रत्याहरेच्चैतदुदितं धौति-कर्म तत् ॥

Dhauti: a wet cloth four fingers wide and fifteen hand spans long is slowly swallowed and withdrawn, as the guru teaches.

verse 2.25कास-श्वास-प्लीह-कुष्ठं कफरोगाश्च विंशतिः ।
धौति-कर्म-प्रभावेण प्रयान्त्येव न संशयः ॥

Dhauti removes cough, asthma, spleen disease, leprosy and twenty mucus disorders.

verse 2.26नाभि-दघ्न-जले पायौ न्यस्त-नालोत्कटासनः ।
आधाराकुञ्चनं कुर्यात्क्षालनं बस्ति-कर्म तत् ॥

Basti: in utkatasana, navel-deep in water, a tube is inserted in the anus and the anus is contracted (washing).

verse 2.27गुल्म-प्लीहोदरं चापि वात-पित्त-कफोद्भवाः ।
बस्ति-कर्म-प्रभावेण क्षीयन्ते सकलामयाः ॥

Basti removes gulma, spleen and abdominal disease, and disorders arising from vata, pitta and kapha.

verse 2.29सूत्रं वितस्ति-सुस्निग्धं नासानाले प्रवेशयेत् ।
मुखान्निर्गमयेच्चैषा नेतिः सिद्धैर्निगद्यते ॥

Neti: a soft thread one span long is passed through the nose and drawn out through the mouth.

verse 2.30कपाल-शोधिनी चैव दिव्य-दृष्टि-प्रदायिनी ।
जत्रूर्ध्व-जात-रोगौघं नेतिराशु निहन्ति च ॥

Neti cleanses the skull, gives clear vision and destroys diseases above the collarbone.

verse 2.31निरीक्षेन्निश्चल-दृशा सूक्ष्म-लक्ष्यं समाहितः ।
अश्रु-सम्पात-पर्यन्तमाचार्यैस्त्राटकं स्मृतम् ॥

Trataka: gaze steadily at a small point, without blinking, until tears come.

verse 2.32मोचनं नेत्र-रोगाणां तन्द्रादीनां कपाटकम् ।
यत्नतस्त्राटकं गोप्यं यथा हाटक-पेटकम् ॥

Trataka removes eye diseases and sloth; it is to be kept secret like a box of gold.

verse 2.33अमन्दावर्त-वेगेन तुन्दं सव्यापसव्यतः ।
नतांसो भ्रामयेदेषा नौलिः सिद्धैः प्रशस्यते ॥

Nauli: bend forward and rotate the abdomen swiftly from side to side like a whirlpool.

verse 2.34मन्दाग्नि-सन्दीपन-पाचनादि-
सन्धापिकानन्द-करी सदैव ।
अशेष-दोष-मय-शोषणी च
हठ-क्रिया मौलिरियं च नौलिः ॥

Nauli kindles weak digestive fire, aids digestion, gives joy, dries up all disorders and is the crown of hatha kriyas.

verse 2.35भस्त्रावल्लोह-कारस्य रेच-पूरौ ससम्भ्रमौ ।
कपालभातिर्विख्याता कफ-दोष-विशोषणी ॥

Kapalabhati: exhalation and inhalation like the bellows of a blacksmith; it dries up phlegm disorders.

verse 2.36षट्-कर्म-निर्गत-स्थौल्य-कफ-दोष-मलादिकः ।
प्राणायामं ततः कुर्यादनायासेन सिद्ध्यति ॥

After the six cleansing kriyas remove fat and mucus, pranayama succeeds without effort.

7Ashta Kumbhaka

verse 2.1अथासने दृढे योगी वशी हित-मिताशनः ।
गुरूपदिष्ट-मार्गेण प्राणायामान्समभ्यसेत् ॥

When asana is firm and the yogi is self-controlled on moderate diet, he should practise pranayama as the guru teaches.

verse 2.2चले वाते चलं चित्तं निश्चले निश्चलं भवेत् ।
योगी स्थाणुत्वमाप्नोति ततो वायुं निरोधयेत् ॥

When the breath moves, the mind moves; when it is still, the mind is still. So restrain the breath.

verse 2.44सूर्य-भेदनमुज्जायी सीत्कारी शीतली तथा ।
भस्त्रिका भ्रामरी मूर्च्छा प्लाविनीत्यष्ट-कुम्भकाः ॥

The eight kumbhakas: suryabheda, ujjayi, seetkari, sheetali, bhastrika, bhramari, moorchha, plavini.

verse 2.48आसने सुखदे योगी बद्ध्वा चैवासनं ततः ।
दक्ष-नाड्या समाकृष्य बहिःस्थं पवनं शनैः ॥

Suryabheda: sit comfortably and draw the outer air in slowly through the right nostril.

verse 2.49आकेशादानखाग्राच्च निरोधावधि कुम्भयेत् ।
ततः शनैः सव्य-नाड्या रेचयेत्पवनं शनैः ॥

Retain until the breath seems to reach the hair roots and nail tips, then exhale slowly through the left nostril.

verse 2.50कपाल-शोधनं वात-दोष-घ्नं कृमि-दोष-हृत् ।
पुनः पुनरिदं कार्यं सूर्य-भेदनमुत्तमम् ॥

Suryabheda purifies the skull, destroys vata disorders and worms; it is to be repeated again and again.

verse 2.51मुखं संयम्य नाडीभ्यामाकृष्य पवनं शनैः ।
यथा लगति कण्ठात्तु हृदयावधि स-स्वनम् ॥

Ujjayi: close the mouth, draw in the breath through both nostrils so the sound is felt from throat to heart.

verse 2.52पूर्ववत्कुम्भयेत्प्राणं रेचयेदिडया तथा ।
श्लेष्म-दोष-हरं कण्ठे देहानल-विवर्धनम् ॥

Retain as before and exhale through the left nostril; it removes phlegm and increases the digestive fire.

verse 2.53नाडी-जलोदराधातु-गत-दोष-विनाशनम् ।
गच्छता तिष्ठता कार्यमुज्जाय्याख्यं तु कुम्भकम् ॥

It removes disorders of the nadis, dropsy and the dhatus; it can be done even while walking or standing.

verse 2.54सीत्कां कुर्यात्तथा वक्त्रे घ्राणेनैव विजृम्भिकाम् ।
एवमभ्यास-योगेन काम-देवो द्वितीयकः ॥

Seetkari: make the hissing “seet” sound with the mouth, breathing in through the mouth; the yogi becomes a second god of love.

verse 2.57जिह्वया वायुमाकृष्य पूर्ववत्कुम्भ-साधनम् ।
शनकैर्घ्राण-रन्ध्राभ्यां रेचयेत्पवनं सुधीः ॥

Sheetali: draw in the air through the tongue, retain as before, and exhale slowly through the nostrils.

verse 2.58गुल्म-प्लीहादिकान्रोगान्ज्वरं पित्तं क्षुधां तृषाम् ।
विषाणि शीतली नाम कुम्भिकेयं निहन्ति हि ॥

Sheetali removes gulma, spleen disorders, fever, excess bile, hunger and thirst, and poisons.

verse 2.59ऊर्वोरुपरि संस्थाप्य शुभे पाद-तले उभे ।
पद्मासनं भवेदेतत्सर्व-पाप-प्रणाशनम् ॥

Bhastrika: sit in padmasana, the feet resting on the thighs; this itself destroys sin.

verse 2.60सम्यक्पद्मासनं बद्ध्वा सम-ग्रीवोदरः सुधीः ।
मुखं संयम्य यत्नेन प्राणं घ्राणेन रेचयेत् ॥

With neck and abdomen in line, close the mouth and exhale through the nose with effort.

verse 2.61यथा लगति हृत्-कण्ठे कपालावधि स-स्वनम् ।
वेगेन पूरयेच्चापि हृत्-पद्मावधि मारुतम् ॥

Breathe out with a sound from heart and throat up to the skull, and fill quickly down to the heart lotus.

verse 2.62पुनर्विरेचयेत्तद्वत्पूरयेच्च पुनः पुनः ।
यथैव लोहकारेण भस्त्रा वेगेन चाल्यते ॥

Exhale and fill again and again, as the blacksmith works the bellows.

verse 2.65विधिवत्कुम्भकं कृत्वा रेचयेदिडयानिलम् ।
वात-पित्त-श्लेष्म-हरं शरीराग्नि-विवर्धनम् ॥

Having done kumbhaka properly, exhale through the left nostril; it removes wind, bile and phlegm and increases the body’s fire.

verse 2.66कुण्डली बोधकं क्षिप्रं पवनं सुखदं हितम् ।
ब्रह्म-नाडी-मुखे संस्थ-कफाद्य्-अर्गल-नाशनम् ॥

It quickly awakens kundalini, is pleasant and beneficial, and destroys the phlegm blocking the mouth of brahma nadi.

verse 2.67सम्यग्गात्र-समुद्भूत-ग्रन्थि-त्रय-विभेदकम् ।
विशेषेणैव कर्तव्यं भस्त्राख्यं कुम्भकं त्विदम् ॥

It pierces the three granthis, and is to be practised with special care.

verse 2.68वेगाद्घोषं पूरकं भृङ्ग-नादं
भृङ्गी-नादं रेचकं मन्द-मन्दम् ।
योगीन्द्राणामेवमभ्यास-योगाच्
चित्ते जाता काचिदानन्द-लीला ॥

Bhramari: inhale with a loud sound like a bee, exhale slowly with a humming sound; the mind fills with joy.

verse 2.69पूरकान्ते गाढतरं बद्ध्वा जालन्धरं शनैः ।
रेचयेन्मूर्च्छाख्येयं मनो-मूर्च्छा सुख-प्रदा ॥

Moorchha: at the end of inhalation, apply jalandhara firmly and exhale slowly; the mind faints into pleasure.

verse 2.70अन्तः प्रवर्तितोदार-मारुतापूरितोदरः ।
पयस्यगाधेऽपि सुखात्प्लवते पद्म-पत्रवत् ॥

Plavini: with the abdomen filled with air, one floats on deep water like a lotus leaf.

verse 2.71प्राणायामस्त्रिधा प्रोक्तो रेच-पूरक-कुम्भकैः ।
सहितः केवलश्चेति कुम्भको द्विविधो मतः ॥

Pranayama is threefold (exhale, inhale, retain); kumbhaka is of two kinds, sahita and kevala.

verse 2.73प्राणायामोऽयमित्युक्तः स वै केवल-कुम्भकः ।
कुम्भके केवले सिद्धे रेच-पूरक-वर्जिते ॥

Kevala kumbhaka: breath held spontaneously, without exhalation or inhalation.

verse 2.75राज-योग-पदं चापि लभते नात्र संशयः ।
कुम्भकात्कुण्डली-बोधः कुण्डली-बोधतो भवेत् ।
अनर्गला सुषुम्णा च हठ-सिद्धिश्च जायते ॥

Through kevala kumbhaka one reaches raja yoga; kundalini awakens, sushumna opens and hatha siddhi arises.
