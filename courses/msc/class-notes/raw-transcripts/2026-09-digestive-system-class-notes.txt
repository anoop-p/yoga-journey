Components of digestive system:
- Alimentary canal (GI (gastro intestinal )Track) - mouth to feces vare Ella Bhagam
	Mouth -> pharynx -> oesophagus ->stomach -> small intestines -> large intestine -> rectum -> anus 
- Accessory organ - helps digestion but food doesn’t pass through them
	ex:- Teeth, tongue, salivary gland, liver, gallbladder, pancreas 

5 major activities of digestion:
*  Ingetion -> digestion -> absorption -> assimilation -> elimination these are the five major activities of digetion 
- Ingestion - Putting/taking food or water to mouth
- Digestion - Breaking complex food into simple substances 
- Absorption - Nutrients move from GI track into blood
- Assimilation - Cells takes up and use absorbed nutrients
- Elimination - Undigested materials expelled as feces (malam)
